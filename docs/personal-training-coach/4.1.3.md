Revalidate restored untouched drafts on changed flag, equipment, readiness, mode, target/history; protect edited/completed rows. Legacy card cannot apply stale recommendation or overwrite edits. 7. Upgrade and rollback from actual 4.1.1, active plan/drafts and new rows; unchanged storage keys/schema; offline, cache, synthetic state only. Full regression root and preview separately, independent reviewed pins/allowlists per 4.1.2, exact candidate SHA artifact, no weakened tests. Do not treat 4.1.1 defect assertions as target acceptance.
8. Final review with all required workflow outcomes, PR draft, staging acceptance and risk report; explicit approval before merge/production. No production files or settings changed. Preview runtime alpha is branch-only until separate publication.
