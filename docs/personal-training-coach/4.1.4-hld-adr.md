## Evidence register
Delavier & Gundill, Strength Training Anatomy Workout (user-provided scan), pp. 28–29: choose resistance with gradual warmup, target repetitions and ease/technique; if target barely achieved repeat, if comfortable consider a small increase. pp. 39–40: log individual sets, adjust load individually, account for fatigue and consistent duration; pp. 33–35, 41–42: trends, overload and recovery. No fixed RIR or session-count thresholds in these passages. No source scans/illustrations committed.
- Currier et al., ACSM 2026 overview of reviews, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/ : progressive resistance training, no consistent benefit of mandatory failure or set structure; healthy adults, broad evidence, not validation of individual algorithm.
- Plotkin et al. 2022, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36199287/ : load vs repetition progression, both viable; specific lower-body study, not validation of any exact threshold.
- Angleri et al. 2017, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28130627/ : equalized-volume ascending pyramid not superior to traditional training in trained men; support preserving chosen structure, not claiming superiority.
- Halperin et al. 2022, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34542869/ : self-predicted repetitions to failure imperfect, use reserve cautiously.
- Robinson et al. 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/ : exploratory meta-regressions using estimated proximity to failure; no universal RIR threshold.
Evidence level: book pages directly reviewed earlier in conversation; publication bibliographic records/abstracts verified; not a systematic review. All numeric thresholds below are product policies, not proven physiological laws.
## Architecture
Existing PWA modular monolith, no service/backend, dependencies from UI -> trainer-preparation/drafts -> single decision policy -> observation/safety/equipment helpers. Keep cardio, timed, assisted and recovery routes separate. Phase A implements pure policy in preview/assistant/intelligent-progression.js, unreferenced by UI; Phase B adapts actual history and integrates existing preparation + legacy card (one authoritative final row decision); Phase C explanations, revalidation, browser tests, cache pins and isolated preview publication subject to authorization. No new data schema in Phase A. No parallel live engine; legacy logic remains active until replacement is integrated and tested.

## Policy (approved, with conservative conflict resolution)
P1 current or historical restriction dominates all increases, including repetitions; missing/invalid data -> hold/review; assisted/unknown equipment, invalid increments and >10% increase blocked for load progression. Valid goals integer 1..10000, RIR integer 0..5. Latest reps above max -> review goal, not automatic increase; below min -> review, no automatic reduction in Phase A. Target RIR 0–1 does not authorize load increase at actual RIR 0–1 (IPE-05 has priority). One observation: at upper limit and actual RIR >= max(3,targetRir+1), use existing calculateNextWeight, target lower bound reps. Two consecutive same-slot same-weight sessions at upper limit with RIR >= max(2,targetRir) allow proposed load increase. At reps within range but below max, RIR >= target+1 -> +1 rep; actual RIR 0–1 no increase (conservative). Avoid new auto reduction until repeated misses, appropriate equipment and scope reviewed; single miss -> review. Stable flat uniform-target existing double progression is retained only with safety gate; reconcile in Phase B, test exact behavior. No guessed free-workout rep range: ask user to set explicit target. For pyramids/top+back-off/mixed, at most one load change per exercise; compare positions, not highest-load duplicates. Prefer latest eligible slot (minimize downstream fatigue); ensure monotonic/equality relations of original adjacent weights preserved (ties stay ties or hold; if no candidate preserving shape, hold). Explicit top/back-off designation not inferred from descending numbers alone. Earlier load change suppresses downstream automatic increases. Mixed structure with ambiguous slot mapping -> hold. Repeated declines and four comparable equal observations -> review signal, not clinical or definitive stagnation; no automatic plan changes. No e1RM change in 4.1.4.
